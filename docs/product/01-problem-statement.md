# Problem Statement & Value Proposition

**Students at a multi-college university cannot tell which announcement, deadline or rule applies to them, and generic AI makes the confusion worse by answering confidently from sources nobody can check.**

The sample knowledge base shows the problem in miniature. One university runs 16 colleges and institutes, dozens of offices spread across seven campus buildings, and more than 25 scholarship types, each with its own endorsing office and document list.

- **Fragmentation.** A student asking "how do I get my tuition exemption?" must find the OLEA steps, then a type-specific requirement list, then the endorsing office (CCA, ADC, HRMO, DILG Makati, OSCA, CIE...), then the office's floor and wing. Each lives in a different document.
- **Missed deadlines.** Announcements and reminders (midterms, enrollment, scholarship applications that must be filed before the semester's fee is paid) reach students through department-by-department channels, so the ones that matter are buried or never seen.
- **Unverified AI answers.** A general chatbot will answer "what GWA do I need for CCIS?" from memory of other universities. A wrong number here costs a student an application.
- **Admin burden.** Department heads re-announce the same policies by hand, and have no view of what students keep asking.

## Why a centralized, college-aligned, strictly grounded assistant is the answer

1. One hub, scoped by college and department, replaces many channels while still showing each student only what applies to them.
2. Every answer is retrieved from official documents and cites its source. If the sources do not contain it, the bot says "Information not in provided sources" instead of guessing.
3. Admins publish a monthly banner or upload a new file in minutes, and the AI's knowledge updates with it. No retraining is involved.
4. Seasonal reminders (Mental Health Awareness Month, midterms week, enrollment deadlines) turn the hub from a lookup tool into something students open without being asked.
